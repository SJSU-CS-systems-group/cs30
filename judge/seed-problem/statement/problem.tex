\problemname{Baby Shark}

\begin{verse}
Baby shark\\
Doo doo doo doo doo doo\\
Baby shark, doo doo doo doo doo doo\\
Baby shark, doo doo doo doo doo doo\\
Baby shark!\\
\end{verse}

(Additional verses refer to Mommy Shark, Daddy Shark, Grandma Shark, Grandpa Shark,
Let’s Go Hunt, Run Away, Safe at Last, and It’s the End.)

Notice that the word ``doo'' is repeated six times consecutively in lines 2, 3, and 4.  
(We ignore case, e.g., the uppercase ``D'' in line 2.)

Given a line of text, determine the maximum number of consecutive repetitions of a word.  
A \emph{word} is defined as any sequence of characters separated by whitespace or by the start or end of the line.

\section*{Input}
The input consists of a single line containing at least one word of English-like text composed of lowercase letters.  
Words are separated by single spaces, and spaces are not part of any word.  
The line contains at most 80 characters.

\section*{Output}
Print the word, in all lowercase, that occurs consecutively the most times.  
If multiple words are tied for the maximum number of consecutive repetitions, print the \textbf{first such word in the input line} (not in alphabetical order).
